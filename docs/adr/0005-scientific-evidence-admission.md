# ADR-0005: Admit scientific comparisons through a frozen native verification boundary

## Status

Accepted.

## Context

Plan scores, native execution and independently verified outcomes answer separate
questions. Some client condition/read controls remain unproven. Executable
presence, successful provider responses and obscure paths do not prove them.

## Decision

Admit gated native comparisons and promotion through one frozen protocol boundary
joining experiment-owned NTM execution, demonstrated condition/read controls,
immutable inputs/work products and protected independent deterministic verification.
Missing or contradictory control, identity, integrity, limit or verifier evidence
blocks or invalidates its comparison/gate. Exploratory development may retain real
execution receipts without isolated comparisons, protected holdouts or promotion.
Analyze all scheduled outcomes; preserve historical evidence meanings.
In this delivery, protected execution and authenticated promotion admission remain
unavailable; promotion export refuses before candidate or output access.
Development-exploratory evidence does not establish isolation or causal skill effects.

## Alternatives considered

- Chosen: frozen admission boundary with client-specific proof and independent checks.
- Rejected: plan scores/provider success, which do not establish loading or task success.
- Rejected: obscure holdout paths, which do not enforce read boundaries.

## Consequences

Evidence is independently assessable, but unknown controls can prevent comparisons
even when providers return outputs. The development pilot remains feasibility proof.
